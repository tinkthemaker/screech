Offline: the whole frame goes to red alert. Buttons that can't do anything go dark.

Spacing rules (every number here is a multiple of 4):

• Gaps between all bar segments and buttons: 4
• Left block: 96 wide; outer curve 48, inner curve 24
• Top bar 12 tall; button bar 28 tall
• Text starts 16 past the inner curve; 12 clear of the bars above and below
• Text: station 22, track 14, status 11; rows 4 apart
• Library rows 28 tall; pills 24 tall; preset pills 20
• Plain text lines up at one left edge; filled rows and pills start 12 further left so their text lands on that same edge
• Inside the console: 12 clear of bars, 8 within a section, 16 between sections; the presets row is a fixed footer 12 above the bottom bar, and tab content ends 16 above it
• Unfolding never moves anything in the top strip; only MORE becomes LESS

Type: Antonio, the free condensed face closest to the show's. Labels and buttons in capitals; station and track names as the stream sends them, so they stay readable.

Colours: the TNG-era palette: orange frame, peach/red/lilac buttons, blue library. Red alert only when offline.
